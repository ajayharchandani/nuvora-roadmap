01 · Executive Summary

Three engines, running at once.

This document expands the founders' plan into an execution-level blueprint — what gets done, in what order, by whom, and with what guardrails — including the legal, tax and compliance steps that are easy to miss in the rush to launch: Import Export Code, GST, trademark protection, a Shareholders' Agreement, shipment insurance, and a compliance calendar that runs from Day 1.

Domestic Soft-Launch
Kirti Nagar sourcing gets Nuvora transacting within 35 days, while the import pipeline is still being built.
Malaysia Import
A ₹40L-capped purchase order lands the differentiated catalog within 75–90 days of kickoff.
Data-Driven Loop
A 40–60 day kill-and-scale cycle repeats for 4–6 months until the China-sourcing trigger (500+ units/month) fires.
